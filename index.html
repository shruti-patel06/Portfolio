A little about me

I'm Shruti, a final-year B.Tech IT student at Vignan's Institute of Engineering for Women. I learn best by experimenting — picking up a technology, building something with it, breaking things along the way, and making it better.

I've explored web development, cloud computing and generative AI, and I haven't settled into one narrow path yet — something I actually enjoy. I'm still discovering which areas of software engineering I want to go deeper into.

Outside of coding, I've been part of UI/UX and technical communities, as a Core Team Member (UI/UX & Web) of the AlgoZenith Club and through hackathons. They taught me that building software isn't just writing code — it's understanding people, communicating ideas and working with others to make them real.

Right now, I'm focused on strengthening my fundamentals, becoming a better problem solver, and gaining the experience to grow as a software developer.
